Our Company

THREE GUYS. ONE VISION.

In 1982, an author, an acupuncturist, and a plumber shared a bold vision for a company that would solely offer premium tobacco products. Bill Drake, the author, wrote a book about tobacco cultivation. His partner was Robert Marlon, an acupuncturist who has more than once been described as a hippie. Eb Wicks was the plumbing contractor who took out a loan to get the startup going in a shed in the Santa Fe Railyard.

The first product was loose tobacco, packed in pouches by hand. Three years later, in 1985, Santa Fe Natural Tobacco Company’s (SFNTC) then-president, Bob Wolf, shepherded our first cigarettes into existence under the name “The Original American Spirit.” In a defining display of their mutual support, the city of Santa Fe celebrated the occasion by naming the day in our honor.

A ROCKY START

For the first few years, the company made little money, shareholders were uneasy, and the doors were almost shut on multiple occasions. Even Bob Wolf was forced to return to his former job manning an Alaskan oil rig for months at a time when the company needed cash.

Meanwhile, a man named Robin Sommers had become increasingly involved with the company. Described by some as a marketing genius, Robin’s initial involvement was from a distance. But by 1986, he was living in Santa Fe, and in 1989 he was named President of SFNTC.

THE ELEMENTS OF SUCCESS

In 1990, the year after Robin took the helm, SFNTC finally eked out a profit.

We believe it was possible to build a cigarette brand on loyalty and trust, moving a premium product by word-of-mouth. Ours was simply a different approach to marketing tobacco at the time – one that has remained key to cultivating and maintaining our consumer and retailer relationships. Most importantly, it’s an approach that adult tobacco consumers would come to respect.

This helped set SFNTC apart as a tobacco company committed to best practices from farm to shelf and proved to be the traction needed to become a pioneer in a centuries-old industry.
