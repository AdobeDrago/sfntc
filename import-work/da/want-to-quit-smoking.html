Want To Quit Smoking?

Smoking is an adult choice. If you choose to stop smoking, there are many resources available to help you achieve your goal. You can start by going to smokefree.gov or calling 1-800-QUIT-NOW for help. We wish you success. If you are on our mailing list and you quit smoking or want us to remove your name for any reason, please contact us so that we can remove your name from our mailing the list. We are committed to only communicating with and advertising to adult smokers aged 21 and over. We never encourage non-smokers to begin smoking or existing smokers to smoke more, and we don’t discourage smokers from quitting.
