Our Commitment

MAKING A DIFFERENCE TOGETHER

Many of us share an aspiration to safeguard and actively improve our planet. Santa Fe Natural Tobacco Company (SFNTC) seeks out ways to live these values through community service, outreach programs, and sharing our knowledge of how to make a meaningful difference for the environment.

Our company has a long-standing commitment to sustainable growing practices, developed over years of partnership with independent growers. The lessons learned along the way help shape our companywide approach to sustainability, taking earth-friendly principles beyond the fields and into communities across America.

We’re constantly discovering unique ways like-minded people are fostering similar ideals in their own towns and neighborhoods. These efforts further inspire us in our pursuit of green initiatives, like reducing cigarette butt litter and refining our earth-friendly packaging.

Every step of the way, our values lead us in crafting the highest quality tobacco blends while helping to secure a lasting future for our communities and the growers with whom we work.
