REDUCING THE MOST LITTERED ITEM IN THE COUNTRY

According to Santa Fe Natural Tobacco Company’s (SFNTC) partner, Keep America Beautiful (KAB), research has shown that even self-reported “non-litterers” often don’t consider tossing cigarette butts on the ground to be “littering”. KAB found that cigarette butt litter occurs most often at transition points, areas where a person must stop smoking before proceeding to another area. These include bus stops, entrances to stores and public buildings, and on the sidewalks outside of bars and restaurants, among others.

To address cigarette butt litter, KAB’s Cigarette Litter Prevention and Recycling Program recommends that communities integrate four proven approaches:

  1. Raise awareness about the issue using public service messages
  2. Place receptacles at transition points such as entrances to public buildings
  3. Distribute pocket or portable ashtrays to adult smokers
  4. Encourage enforcement of litter laws, including cigarette litter

Additionally, there are 3 ways you can help reduce cigarette butt litter:

  1. KAB provides information about starting and maintaining a Cigarette Litter Prevention and Recycling Program in your community, and can be found online here.
  2. You can also get involved by signing up as a member of the Cigarette Waste Recycling Program, a national, cigarette-waste recycling program run by TerraCycle with funding from SFNTC. Learn how you can help.
  3. SFNTC also provides free butt pouches for collecting and recycling cigarette butts for adult tobacco consumers which can be requested at AmericanSpirit.com
