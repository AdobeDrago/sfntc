HOW WE GET FROM SEED TO CIGARETTE

When we started Santa Fe Natural Tobacco Company (SFNTC) in 1982, we only sold roll-your-own tobacco. “Manufacturing” consisted of blending and cutting loose tobacco to package into plastic bags. We’ve come a long way since then, tackling an array of challenges on the road toward manufacturing cigarettes, as well as roll-your-own tobacco blends in pouches and tins. We’ve learned that perfection takes persistence.

At the beginning, we ripped up imperfect cigarettes by hand because we didn’t own a tobacco-reclaiming machine. Our machines were not state-of-the-art, so we had more imperfect cigarettes than we expected. Necessity was the mother of invention, as our employees found a way to convert a butter bean sheller into a machine for reclaiming tobacco. We were proud to be able to reuse old equipment for a new purpose, and even more so that we did it all ourselves.

We now have modern, high-speed machines to make and pack cigarettes, but our same commitment to the highest quality product still remains throughout our manufacturing process.

From the time a tobacco leaf leaves the grower’s hands to when it rolls out of the manufacturing plant in a finished product, a complete, thorough and fully-documented audit trail helps ensure the integrity of our tobacco supply.

At each step of processing and manufacturing, organic tobacco must be handled correctly and in accordance with strict National Organic Program (NOP) standards. It’s critically important that organic tobacco is not mixed with any other tobacco, ensuring it remains free of materials and substances not approved by the NOP. Our adherence to the stringent NOP standards makes the manufacturing process more challenging for us than most other tobacco companies, but it is well worth it as part of our commitment to quality.

Our manufacturing lines must be cleaned according to a specific regimen before running any organic tobacco through them. Entire machines, some stretching as long as half a football field, must be extensively cleaned.

It’s important to have the ability to isolate potential problems with our products quickly and efficiently. Each finished product has a tracking code which we can use to find information all the way back to the farm where the tobacco was grown.

Integrity and quality are paramount in all of our practices at SFNTC, and manufacturing is no different. Our high-quality tobacco products are a direct result of our high standards, maintained by employees who take pride in their work.
