Transforming Tobacco

AT THE FOREFRONT OF CHANGE

We’ve been going against the grain of convention since 1982.

Santa Fe Natural Tobacco Company (SFNTC) was founded on principles of quality, authenticity, and environmental sustainability. At the time, our vision of a tobacco product made from high quality, whole-leaf tobacco was revolutionary.

That vision is carried out in our commitment to sustainability, including a dedication to continuous improvement in lessening our impact on the planet. Encouraging recycling and energy conservation in our facilities, creating more sustainable packaging, and working to make earth-friendly tobacco growing more economical: these initiatives all play a vital role in bringing our vision to life.

This commitment includes a dedication to helping to prevent youth smoking. We distribute marketing materials only to adult tobacco smokers 21 years of age and older and offer age verification materials to retailers.

We’re continuously growing our relationships with our employees, adult consumers, customers, and communities. Our community extends beyond our family at the workplace to include the many people whom we assist with our employee outreach projects and philanthropic commitments.
