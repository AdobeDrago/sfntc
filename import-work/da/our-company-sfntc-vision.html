THE ART OF A HANDCRAFTED VISION

The picture you see above isn’t necessarily pretty, and some may not even deem it frame-worthy. The story behind its creation, however, is a perfect picture of why it’s not only our company vision, but essential to everything we do.

In the mid-2000s, company leaders wanted to more clearly define who we are, who we want to be, and ultimately build an all-encompassing vision for the future of Santa Fe Natural Tobacco Company (SFNTC) based on our values.

We knew we already had a solid foundation of company principles upon which to build:

  • We have a strong commitment to premium products, made with high quality whole-leaf tobacco.
  • We believe in being environmentally responsible.
  • We build and maintain strong relationships within our company and the communities in which we work.

We nurtured these relationships by giving each and every SFNTC employee a voice in the crafting of our company vision. It took a significant amount of energy, thought, and emotion from everyone involved, but the arduous piece of artwork you see above was well worth the effort.

After hours of long interviews and intense brainstorming sessions with our employees, one clear and concise vision for the future of SFNTC became apparent. The words of our final vision statement continue to resonate and guide us into the future:

“The people of Santa Fe Natural Tobacco Company share a values-drive vision: that our uncompromising commitment to our real tobacco products, the earth from which they come, the communities on which we depend, and the people who bring our spirit to life are essential to our success.”
