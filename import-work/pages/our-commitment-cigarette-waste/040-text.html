TerraCycle CEO and founder Tom Szaky says, “The Cigarette Waste Recycling Program provides a way to recycle the filters that are properly disposed of in an ashtray or can, but still end up in a landfill.”

Contrary to popular belief, cigarette butts do not break down quickly. A Keep America Beautiful® study found that 65% of all cigarette butts are disposed of improperly. The SFNTC-supported Cigarette Waste Recycling Program provides a way to keep cigarette waste from filling landfills.

In the past, SFNTC had flower pots made from recycled materials that include recycled cigarette butts, demonstrating our passion for turning litter into something beautiful. The pots were sent out with seeded paper and dirt, so recipients could easily start growing wildflowers of their own.

In 2018, we challenged teams in 36 communities across the country to clean up and recycle cigarette butts to celebrate Earth Day. Each of those communities received a flower pot or park bench made from recycled materials, including cigarette waste, in recognition of their efforts.
