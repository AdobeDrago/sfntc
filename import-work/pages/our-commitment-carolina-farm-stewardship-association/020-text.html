LEADING THE WAY FOR CAROLINAS’ ORGANIC FARMERS

We’re proud to show our support for CFSA, celebrating more than 43 years of advocacy for locally-grown food and the farmers who grow it.

With our contributions, CFSA is able to support:

  • Education about sustainable and organic growing practices.
  • The economic health of numerous agrarian-based North Carolina communities where tobacco continues to be a very important crop.
  • Farmers who may be interested in transitioning to organic growing, potentially increasing the pool of organic growers for many crops.
  • A better environment, because environmental stewardship is a core focus of CFSA.

In addition to helping the sustainability of tobacco crops, CFSA also helps ensure the sustainability of the food supply by creating new markets for organic wheat: considered a perfect rotation crop to reinvigorate farmland where tobacco is grown.

A prime example of CFSA’s work is its development of Carolina Ground Flour, a cooperatively owned small-scale milling operation which produces organic bread flour. This project is designed to connect farmers with local bakeries and restaurants, creating a tangible level of security and sustainability for farmers while providing customers with superior, locally-sourced flour.

This initiative aligns directly with our commitment to farmers, as wheat grown in rotation with tobacco helps sustain the land and replenish the soil. With this knowledge, dozens of organic farmers who grow tobacco for Santa Fe Natural Tobacco Company’s products were able to implement a commercially viable rotation and cover crop for their fields.

All of the organic farmers whom we contract to grow tobacco for us use wheat in rotation with tobacco in their fields. CFSA’s development of an organic wheat market in North Carolina was a crucial piece of the puzzle that made this possible.
