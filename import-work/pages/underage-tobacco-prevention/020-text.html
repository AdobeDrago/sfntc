OUR COMMITMENT TO PREVENTING UNDERAGE ACCESS TO TOBACCO

At Santa Fe Natural Tobacco Company (SFNTC), we believe youth should not smoke or use any form of tobacco, and this principle is deeply ingrained in our company culture.

Since our founding in 1982, our company has taken its corporate and civic responsibilities seriously by implementing programs and actively supporting efforts to prevent underage access to Natural American Sprit or any other tobacco products.

In keeping with our commitment to underage tobacco prevention, we do not market our products to people under age 21 and we actively support retailer and other efforts to prevent underage access to tobacco products.

Beyond that, as a matter of long-standing policy and practice, we never encourage non-smokers to start, or existing smokers to smoke more, and we don’t discourage smokers from quitting.

The 1998 Master Settlement Agreement (MSA), which comprehensively addresses the major issues related to underage smoking and cigarette marketing in the United States, requires SFNTC to find ways to reduce underage smoking. We have been doing this for many years, and our efforts to prevent underage tobacco use predate the requirements in the MSA.

Some facts about SFNTC’s commitment to preventing underage access to tobacco:

  • SFNTC requiring retailers to ID-check purchasers up to the age of 27.
  • SFNTC has, since 1998, annually provided Age Reference materials to retailers to inform and remind both sales people and customers of the legal age to smoke in a given state.
  • SFNTC has regularly affirmed through communications with its adult smokers, and in numerous public statements, that smoking is not appropriate for youth.
  • SFNTC has long refrained from placing advertising in magazines and newspapers that have more than 15% under 21 readership.
  • SFNTC reviews advertising and promotional materials to ensure that their content meets our obligations, including our long-standing commitment to help prevent underage tobacco use.
  • SFNTC requires age verification before adult smokers gain access to our Natural American Spirit marketing website and are added to its database to receive direct-marketing materials.
