HOW WE GET FROM SEED TO CIGARETTE

When we started Santa Fe Natural Tobacco Company (SFNTC) in 1982, we only sold roll-your-own tobacco. “Manufacturing” consisted of blending and cutting loose tobacco to package into plastic bags. We’ve come a long way since then, tackling an array of challenges on the road toward manufacturing cigarettes, as well as roll-your-own tobacco blends in pouches and tins. We’ve learned that perfection takes persistence.

At the beginning, we ripped up imperfect cigarettes by hand because we didn’t own a tobacco-reclaiming machine. Our machines were not state-of-the-art, so we had more imperfect cigarettes than we expected. Necessity was the mother of invention, as our employees found a way to convert a butter bean sheller into a machine for reclaiming tobacco. We were proud to be able to reuse old equipment for a new purpose, and even more so that we did it all ourselves.
