AN OVERVIEW OF OUR WHOLE-LEAF TOBACCO BLEND PRODUCTS

Santa Fe Natural Tobacco Company (SFNTC) is the manufacturer and marketer of Natural American Spirit cigarettes and roll-your-own tobacco products. Natural American Spirit makes fourteen cigarette styles and four roll-your-own styles with whole-leaf tobacco blends.

Our blenders create our cigarette and roll-your-own tobacco blends using only two ingredients: whole-leaf tobacco and water. Additionally, Natural American Spirit makes certain styles of cigarettes and roll-your-own tobacco using 100% U.S. grown, made with organic tobacco.

In 1989, we began pioneering growing programs for organic tobacco. We worked directly with independent farmers to grow tobacco according to organic standards. We encouraged these contracted growers to meet the rigorous USDA National Organic Program certification process when it was established in 2000. Every year we expand our relationships with growers to educate them about organic growing methods.

Our Tobaccoville, NC facility manufactures the blends for all Natural American Spirit products in the US.

MARKETING PHILOSOPHY

At Santa Fe Natural Tobacco Company we try to operate in as transparent a manner as possible. Therefore, our advertising is simple, straightforward, and information-based, with the goal of educating smokers about our premium whole-leaf tobacco products.

Santa Fe Natural Tobacco Company believes that smoking is an adult choice. Therefore, we market only to smokers 21 years of age or older. Each year, we offer retailers updated “Age Reference” signs to help prevent sales to minors and remind the retailers to verify the age of persons purchasing Natural American Spirit or any other tobacco products.

Santa Fe Natural Tobacco Company is a signatory to the Master Settlement Agreement (MSA) and as such we meet all our obligations and make all required payments.

We don’t encourage non-smokers to start smoking; we don’t discourage smokers from quitting, nor do we encourage smokers to smoke more.
